## Verification

Source packages are rebuilt with Solidity 0.8.25 and compared against the recorded mainnet runtime hashes, including metadata. UUPS self-addresses and staking/strategy immutable constants are resolved before comparison; public immutable getters are checked separately. Proxy pointers, creation transactions, administration roles and recorded configuration reads are verified at the pinned block. This is source and deployment verification, not a new security audit or a funded end-to-end strategy certification.

The USDC pool holds the external [USDC token](https://explorer.horizen.io/address/0xdf7108f8b10f9b9ec1aba01cca057268cbf86b6c). ZEN custody uses the external [ZEN token](https://explorer.horizen.io/address/0x57da2d504bf8b83ef304759d9f2648522d7a9280). Imported interfaces and OpenZeppelin libraries are compilation dependencies, not claims of additional deployed Layrs contracts.
